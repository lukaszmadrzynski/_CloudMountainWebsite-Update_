  days:
  - day: 1
    title: Welcome to Kunming
    schedule:
    - period: ''
      activities:
      - Morning arrival at Kunming Changshui Airport or Train Station, transfer to your hotel, time to refresh and a quiet first walk as you settle into the 1,900m plateau. As afternoon slips into golden hour, stroll the willow-lined paths of Green Lake Park - the cultural soul of modern Kunming and the ancient heartland of the Dian Kingdom, Yunnan's first recorded civilization (2nd century BCE). Welcome dinner at a traditional Yunnan food restaurant in Kunming Old Town, the kind of slow food that sets the tone for the next seven days.
  - day: 2
    title: High-Speed Train to Dali + Erhai Lake
    schedule:
    - period: ''
      activities:
      - Board the high-speed train at Kunming Station. Two hours of Yunnan rolling past the window - rice paddies, red-earth hills, the Cangshan Mountain range drawing closer. Arrive in Dali for lunch, then an afternoon on Erhai Lake's eastern shore - an optional 12-km bike ride (or shorter walk) through fields, Bai villages, and lakeside lanes. Bai Minority dinner and a wander through Dali Old Town - the former capital of the Nanzhao Kingdom, all cobblestone alleys, Bai carved-stone facades, and a string of quiet teahouses.
  - day: 3
    title: Shibaoshan Grottoes + Shaxi + Lijiang
    schedule:
    - period: ''
      activities:
      - Drive into the Jianchuan hills to Mount Shibaoshan - a sacred mountain with 1,000-year-old Buddhist grottoes carved into the cliff face, plus a Bai-shrine complex still in active pilgrimage use. A slow afternoon in Shaxi - one of the best-preserved Tea Horse Road market towns in Yunnan. Stone bridges over the Heihui River, the centuries-old market square with traditional theater stage, alleys still used by horse traders' descendants. Late-afternoon drive north through the highland country, watching the light change as you climb toward Lijiang. Check into your hotel and finish the day with a Naxi minority dinner in the Old Town.
  - day: 4
    title: Lijiang In-Depth
    schedule:
    - period: ''
      activities:
      - 'Start your day at the Lijiang Heritage Museum for a focused Naxi history and Dongba culture intro, then walk into Black Dragon Pond Park (UNESCO Site) for the full Jade Dragon Snow Mountain views. Enter the cobblestone lanes of Dayan Old Town for a proper bowl of the legendary crossing-bridge noodles. After lunch, heritage workshops: draw Dongba pictographs, craft traditional paper, follow a guided scavenger hunt through the backstreets. End the afternoon at the Mu Palace, the former seat of the Naxi chieftains. Unhurried Naxi minority specialty dinner, then a one-hour Naxi Ancient Music performance in a historic courtyard. Close the night with a stroll around the lit-up Old Town night market.'
  - day: 5
    title: Lashi Wetlands + Tiger Leaping Gorge + Shangri-La
    schedule:
    - period: ''
      activities:
      - 'Dawn at the Lashi Plateau Wetland Reserve (2,440m) to observe rare birds and learn about this vital ecosystem. Then drive north to the legendary Tiger Leaping Gorge with spectacular views of Jade Dragon Snow Mountain, one of the deepest river canyons on Earth. Drive on toward Shangri-La through increasingly Tibetan country - prayer flags on the slopes, yaks on the road. Late afternoon: a quiet wander through Dukezong Old Town - white-washed Tibetan homes, prayer wheels, the famous 21-metre prayer wheel spinning at the heart of town. Finish the day with a Tibetan feast and traditional music.'
  - day: 6
    title: Songzanlin + Tangka + Train to Kunming
    schedule:
    - period: ''
      activities:
      - A morning at the magnificent Songzanlin Monastery - the 'Little Potala Palace', the most important Tibetan Buddhist monastery in Yunnan. Then a hands-on afternoon with a Songzanlin-affiliated artist learning the basics of Tangka - the intricate Tibetan Buddhist painting tradition. Mineral pigments, gold-line work, and a one-piece take-home. A cultural experience unique to Tibetan Yunnan - not a souvenir shop. All aboard for the fast train back to Kunming – 4-5 hours of highland light fading into the lowland dusk. A quiet meal on board and a final look back at the mountains.
  - day: 7
    title: Kunming Farewell
    schedule:
    - period: ''
      activities:
      - Free time for last wanderings, a final Yunnan coffee, the things you didn't get to on Day 1. Then a private transfer to Kunming Changshui Airport for the flight home. Safe travels - and a piece of Yunnan to take with you.

We hope this journey will not only create unforgettable memories but also result in a deeper understanding of challenges and solutions to a more sustainable future.
